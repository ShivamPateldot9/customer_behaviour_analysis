# Customer Purchase Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes a retail customer transactions dataset to uncover insights around revenue, customer segmentation, discounts, shipping preferences, subscriptions, and product performance. Data was cleaned in **Python**, analyzed in **PostgreSQL**, and visualized in a **Power BI** dashboard. The goal was to answer real-world business questions that a data analyst is commonly asked to solve.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- **Python (Pandas)**: data cleaning and exploration before loading into SQL
- **SQL (PostgreSQL)**: aggregate functions, `GROUP BY`, `CASE WHEN`, subqueries, CTEs, `ROW_NUMBER()` window function
- **Power BI**: dashboard visualizing key findings from the query outputs

## 🗂️ Dataset
A single `customer` table with transaction-level retail data, including: `customer_id`, `gender`, `age_group`, `item_purchased`, `category`, `purchase_amount`, `discount_applied`, `review_rating`, `shipping_type`, `subscription_status`, `previous_purchases`.

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
1. Total revenue generated by male vs. female customers
2. Customers who used a discount but still spent above the average purchase amount
3. Top 5 products by average review rating
4. Average purchase amount: Standard vs. Express shipping
5. Do subscribed customers spend more? (average spend and total revenue comparison)
6. Top 5 products with the highest discount usage rate
7. Customer segmentation: New / Returning / Loyal (based on purchase history)
8. Top 3 most purchased products within each category (window functions)
9. Do repeat buyers (5+ previous purchases) show higher subscription rates?
10. Revenue contribution by age group

## 💡 Key Insights
- Male customers generate about 68% of total revenue, roughly 2.1x that of female customers
- Revenue is spread evenly across age groups (24-27% each), with Young Adults contributing the most at 26.7%
